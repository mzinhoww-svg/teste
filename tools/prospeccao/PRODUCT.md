# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Letícia Reiners, who runs prospecting and post-sale for Reiners Media (a podcast and institutional video studio in Cuiabá). She works mostly on a notebook at the studio or office, going through the day's queue: opening each lead, sending the WhatsApp or e-mail touch, marking what went out and logging what happened. Between meetings she checks the same central on her phone to send a pending touch or read a lead before a call.

## Product Purpose
The central de disparo is the single place where the outbound cadence (visit, free diagnosis, pilot episode) and the post-sale flow (kickoff to recurrence) are worked lead by lead. It holds each lead's research (what the business does, who leads it, direct contacts, Receita data, alerts) and history, so Letícia can personalise each message and never lose track of who was contacted, when, and what is next. Success is hitting the daily touch goal (20) with messages that reach the decision-maker, without re-researching anyone.

## Brand Personality
Operational, dense, fast. It is an internal working tool that carries the Reiners identity (navy and gold, institutional and precise) without decoration. It should feel like a well-kept ledger: everything needed for the next action is on screen, nothing is there for show.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive): purple-blue gradients, identical card grids, dashboard hero metrics.
- Overloaded screens where every element competes; it must be readable at a glance.
- Small, low-contrast text: muted gray on near-white, labels that are hard to read on a phone outdoors.

## Design Principles
- The next action first: each lead shows what to send today and the one button that sends it before any background.
- Density with hierarchy: show a lot per screen on desktop, but with one clear primary per card and quiet secondary detail.
- Research at hand, not in the way: profile, contacts and history are one click from the card, never a separate screen.
- Trust what was sent: every send, undo, reply and note is recorded with its date and visible in the lead's history.
- Desktop-first, phone-ready: wide working layout on the notebook that collapses cleanly to one column on the phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for all text, including labels and placeholders, in light and dark themes. Body text at least 14px, readable on a phone in daylight. Full keyboard operation with visible focus. Respect reduced motion.
